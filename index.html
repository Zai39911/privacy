Privacy Policy – Cricket World Mania

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the "Cricket Bot" app used by the Facebook Page Cricket World Mania. The app is used only by the Page owner to automatically publish daily cricket score updates to the Page.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information from Facebook users or Page visitors. It only uses public cricket match data (team names, scores and results) from a third-party cricket data service.

How the app uses Facebook

The app uses the Facebook Graph API only to publish posts to the Cricket World Mania Page, which is owned and managed by the app owner. It does not read, store, or process data from any other users or Pages.

Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

Data sharing

No personal data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Data deletion

The app does not store any user data, so there is no user data to delete. If you have interacted with the Cricket World Mania Page and want any of your information removed, or want to ask a question, email titanssports3991@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
